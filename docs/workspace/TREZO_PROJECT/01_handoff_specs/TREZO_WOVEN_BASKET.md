## 4. KINDRIP — GENERATIONAL WEALTH

### The Name
- **KIN** from "kindred"
- **DRIP** from "Dividend Reinvestment Plan"
- KINDRIP = Building wealth across generations through automated dividend transfer

### The Core Concept
Instead of dividends and profits flowing only to the parent account, KINDRIP routes a percentage directly to linked child accounts in real time. Every trade the parent makes becomes an investment in their child's future.

### The Tagline

## 11. LEGAL & TAX FRAMEWORK

### Custodial Account Structure
- UGMA (Uniform Gifts to Minors Act) or UTMA (Uniform Transfers to Minors Act)
- Parent acts as custodian
- Account belongs to child legally
- Custodianship transfers at age of majority (varies by state)

### Tax Implications
- "Kiddie Tax" rules apply for unearned income
- First $1,300/year (2025): Tax-free
- $1,301-$2,600: Taxed at child's rate (usually 10%)
- Over $2,600: Taxed at parent's rate
- Trezo's Tax Optimizer Agent handles all calculations

### Custodianship Transfer
- Age of majority typically 18 or 21 (state-dependent)
- Trezo provides transfer assistance
- Educational materials about adult financial responsibility
