## Changes in this patch

- Use completed consolidated daily volume for stock liquidity screening,
  with explicit availability failures and unchanged numeric risk limits.
  Keep strategy price bars and real-time quote feeds separate.
- Honor explicitly declared crypto signals at the stock-only gate.
- Include bounded rejection reasons and book attribution in approval alerts.
  Per-book refusal totals no longer claim to prove every approval had an
  outcome. Correlated approval accounting remains a separate improvement.
- Capture sanitized kill-switch failure categories within each request.
  Unreadable or malformed state still blocks execution; errors never become
  an empty healthy account list.
- Require stop evidence to close the position's side. Surface unexpected
  protection-check exceptions without exception text, and avoid consuming
  the protection cooldown while the stock market is closed.

Alpaca documents IEX/SIP coverage and the historical SIP delay requirement:
https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq
